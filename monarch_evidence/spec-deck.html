§1

Monarch models evidence in five places, five ways

None of them answers the question every downstream consumer asks: how strongly, and in which direction, does the evidence bear on this claim, and who decided that?

DisMech

No interpretation provenance

An evidence vocabulary, and no record of who did the interpreting. The resource is AI-curated.

MeDIC

No evidence model

Provenance to a high standard. No direction slot at all: one implicit SUPPORTS.

mondo-ai

No variation

Already on SEPIO. Its aggregate is 1.0 across all 160,187 packets, because nothing can emit a disputes line.

Two constraints on Track 1

Simple and intuitive

Track 1 has to be pitchable across Monarch for adoption to happen at all. Every slot must earn its place.

No overfitting

No class is shaped around one pipeline's output. A slot proposed by one resource is checked against the other eight records before it enters the kernel.

§3

the span
DataItem
>
the source
Document

EvidenceLine is the output of an interpretation act: an agent reading items as evidence. It carries direction, strength, and the provenance of that reading.

DataItem exists independently of being interpreted. It carries its own provenance (who produced it), and can be used as evidence for other claims.

§3

Three consequences of the nesting

01

Two provenances

The item records who created the data. The line records who read those items as supporting the claim, when, and under which guideline. Collapsing item into line loses the second, which is the axis AI curation needs.

02

Items are reusable

The same span can be evidence for one claim and against another. Making it an object rather than a field is what allows that.

03

Lines may be heterogeneous

An EvidenceLine is a line of reasoning, so grouping several kinds of item under one direction is within SEPIO. Split lines only when the reasoning differs.

§3 · Q2

Item reuse: the objection, and the data

the objection

The extra nesting costs: Python classes and validators get deeper, and data creators can misuse the freedom. In practice it is cheaper to mint a new item than to find and reuse an existing one.

what the corpus shows

D1 has DisMech duplicating PMID:25604431 four times, with four different snippets. Every pipeline in the corpus mints fresh items. None reuses.

Interpretation provenance is what justifies the item/line split, and it is exercised by seven of the nine examples. Reuse is not doing that work.

§3.1

EvidenceItem is a role, DataItem is a class

the role

EvidenceItem

Any information content entity can play it: a text span, a study result, a dataset, a computational result, a curator's contribution.

the class

DataItem

A text span is a DataItem with a value (the quoted text) and a reported_in (the document).

SEPIO subclasses further only when a use case demands it.

The evidence model does not hold the claim. The host holds it already, as a triple or as a field on a record, and the microschema only hangs evidence lines onto it. Reading the axis diagram top down suggests otherwise, because it starts at Statement.

§4.4

Two readings of that table

reading 1

Only four of twenty kinds have a span

Kinds 1, 2, 3 and 10, which are also most of the volume. The other sixteen are a record, a number, a mapping, or a judgement. A kernel whose only concrete item class is a text span forces those sixteen to invent a quote. That is the N1 concordance finding, generalised.

reading 2

Four of them are not items at all

Kinds 12, 13, 16 and 17 are interpretation acts: an agent read something and reached a verdict. Under §3 and §7.1 that is what an EvidenceLine is. Modelling them as items puts the agent on the wrong side of arrow 2.

has_evidence_items has to range over the abstract EvidenceItem, not over a concrete text class.

§4.5

Expert opinion and evidence synthesis are not the same thing

second-order

Evidence synthesis

Always an evaluation of concrete pieces of evidence. Its inputs are other items or lines, it carries cited_evidence, and it is defeated by attacking those inputs. N3's LLM verdict is this. So is the verdict D2 needs over 3 supporting and 5 refuting items.

first-order

Expert opinion

Need not rest on anything retrievable. A clinician of twenty years asserts "A is a treatment for B". They may not be able to say which paper taught them, and there may be no paper. It is defeated by attacking the expert, not the inputs, because there are none.

The operational rule: if it cites evidence it is a synthesis, if it does not it is testimony. Checkable, and it decides which slot the number goes in. A synthesis score is computed over inputs. A testimony's weight comes from TrustLevel on the asserting agent (R12).

§4.5 · Q11 closed

The testimony is itself the item

An earlier draft worried that expert review with nothing underneath produces a line with zero items. The premise was wrong. An assertion is a real information artefact: it has a value (what was said), an author, and sometimes a document (a guideline, a meeting record, a personal communication).

TestimonyEvidenceItem, class_uri sepio:DataItem
has_evidence_lines:
  - direction_of_evidence_provided: SUPPORTS
    strength_of_evidence_provided: MODERATE   from TrustLevel on the agent, not from a citation
    has_evidence_items:
      - value: "A is a treatment for B"
        asserted_by: orcid:0000-0002-...
        traceability: UNTRACEABLE          ECO:0000034

Every line keeps at least one item, and the kernel needs no zero-item special case.

§4.2

Shapes A and C are one class

shape A

Only the required slots

value plus reported_in: Document. The always-valid minimal item. A verbatim sentence is a fact about the document, true whether or not anyone extracted it.

shape C

The extraction slots filled in

Extraction score, span role, method, and the ordered grounding chain. Every one is optional, so an instance carrying only value and reported_in validates identically to an A.

Both are TextDerivedEvidenceItem (§4.5). C is implemented in mondo-ai as TextMiningResult and not yet in minimal.yaml. It is where the grounding chain lands (§7.3).

§4.3

What Track 1 omits

no Agentno Contributionno curation statusno synthesisno scoringno item identity beyond an optional id

Keeping these out is what makes Track 1 pitchable. A resource that needs any of them is on Track 2 for that part of its model.

Open tension (Q3). Interpretation provenance, meaning who read this span as supporting the claim and with which model, is what makes AI-curated evidence trustworthy, and it sits on the Track 2 side of the line. DisMech is AI-curated and records none of it. If Track 1 stays agent-free, every AI-curated resource reaches into Track 2 immediately, which weakens the case for starting simple.

§7

Three concepts, not two

Question it answersTerritory
ProvenanceWhere did this record come from, and how was it produced?W3C PROV; MeDIC's transformation chain
EvidenceWhat are the reasons to believe or disbelieve the claim?SEPIO; has polarity and weight
Data qualityHow faithfully does this record represent its source?the derivative of provenance

A resource can be excellent at one and have none of another. MeDIC has best-in-class provenance and no evidence model. DisMech has a rich evidence vocabulary and almost no interpretation provenance. These are different gaps and need different work.

§7

Classifying any field, in three questions

question 1

Does it survive if the claim turns out to be false?

provenance

question 2

Would it change if a different pipeline produced an identical record?

provenance or data quality
never evidence

question 3

Would an expert who trusts your pipeline completely still want to see it?

§7

Every confidence MeDIC computes is a data-quality number

0.855

on the gemifloxacin record means "we are 85.5% confident we linked the right CHEBI id".

It does not mean there is an 85.5% chance the claim is true.

the silent conversion

Mapped onto score_of_evidence_provided

"unsure of the identifier" becomes "weak evidence for the treatment". This is the most dangerous integration error available here.

Chain confidence is a gate on the line, never the line's score.

§7.1

Neither quality number may become a line score

arrow 1

Chain confidence is a gate on the line, never the line's score.

arrow 2

What belongs on the line is the organisation's declared, dated, revisable trust in the interpreter.

N3 has this the wrong way round. An LLM's self-reported 0.95 is stored as a Score, in the same slot type as a computed Net Evidence Ratio, and no consumer can tell them apart.

Part five

Two open questions

Both concern where extraction machinery belongs.

§7.2 · Q8

Should the base evidence item be an extraction result?

the case for
  • Puts arrow-1 provenance on every item by construction.
  • Unifies human and machine curation: a curator quoting a paper is an extractor with a human agent.
the case against
  • It overfits. N1's concordance extracts nothing: three ontologies independently carry the string. N2's consortium decision is not an extraction either.
  • It taxes the simple case. The adoption pitch becomes "your quote is a DataExtractionResult".
  • The middle level is unoccupied. In all nine examples the span and the document are adjacent.

The relayed proposal appears to collapse DataItem into Document. Clarify what the intermediate level holds before arguing the case.

§8.1

Three ways a naive tally goes wrong

R14 · supersession

Counting superseded evidence

DisMech records AMX0035 with one SUPPORT item (phase-2 CENTAUR) and two REFUTE items (phase-3 PHOENIX, and the 2024 withdrawal). A tally reads 1:2. The phase-3 result supersedes the phase-2 result, and the withdrawal is a consequence rather than a third vote.

R15 · defeaters

Counting admissibility as counter-evidence

An argument that evidence does not bear on the claim is a different kind of thing from an argument that the claim is false.

R20 · independence

Counting republications as corroboration

MeDIC's noisy-OR reads six documents as six independent authorities and reaches 0.999998. The effective count is four. MeDIC's own reliability tier for the same pair is MEDIUM.

All three are needed to compute an aggregate that is not misleading. All three are SEPIO questions rather than local ones.

D3 · R15

The undercutting defeater

The Rnf170-null mouse develops an ADSA-like sensory phenotype, which reads as strong MODEL_ORGANISM support for a loss-of-function mechanism.

Heterozygous human null carriers are unaffected, and homozygotes get a different disease. The null mouse corresponds to the recessive condition, not the dominant one, so the mouse evidence is inadmissible for this claim.

what a naive NER does

Counts it as one more REFUTES line about the mechanism.

its actual effect

Removes a SUPPORTS line from the tally.

Direction is only meaningful relative to a named target. The same span is REFUTE against the ADSA mechanism claim and supports the discussion's own conclusion, which is why direction lives on the line and not on the item.

§8 · what the corpus establishes

Four findings

  • Direction is the biggest single win available. MeDIC has none, and mondo-ai's cannot vary. Both compute aggregates that are mathematically incapable of moving.
  • The three-way split is not academic. M1, M2, M3 and N2 each contain at least one number that would be badly misread if mapped to the wrong concept.
  • The normalised representation earns its keep at D3 and N3, not at D1. The argument for separate items and lines is that interpretation is a separate act with its own agent, date, method and defeasibility.
  • Three requirements have no representation anywhere: supersession, defeaters, source independence.

summary

Where this leaves the two tracks

  • Track 1 is drafted and validates. R23, R24 and R25 are met today; what is missing is guidance.
  • The item/line split is justified by interpretation provenance, not by reuse.
  • The statement stays in the host, structured, and statement_text is a label.
  • Quality attaches to two arrows and belongs in neither evidence-strength slot.
  • The base item stays TextSpan; the grounding chain goes on shape C.
  • Supersession, defeaters and source independence go to SEPIO as questions.

Blockers B1 (kernel id) and B2 (real ECO and publication-type IRIs) gate broad adoption. Q8 needs one clarification from Matt before it can be closed.
