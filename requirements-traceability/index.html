    Requirements traceability: Mondo requirements to the SIEVE profile¶

    Each requirement from the Mondo requirements collection is assigned to a SEPIO design module and a disposition (requirements/traceability.yaml). The schema elements listed for a requirement are those annotated with its ID in schema/sieve.yaml.

    SEPIO design modules¶

    Module SEPIO core classes Description
    Statement (claim semantics) (statement) Statement, Proposition, Qualifier What is claimed: SPO(Q) on the Statement, claim-type profiles, negation, and the Statement-level assessment (direction / strength / score in SEPIO's "proposition assessment mode").
    Evidence Item (evidence-item) InformationEntity, Document, DataItem, DataSet, StudyResult The information interpreted as evidence: external records, prior claims, documents, computed scores; and the information resources they come from.
    Evidence Line (interpretation) (evidence-line) EvidenceLine How evidence bears on a claim: evidence lines with direction, strength and score, their validity, and the shortcut links (has_evidence, has_evidence_from_sources).
    Provenance (activities, contributions, agents, methods) (provenance) Activity, Contribution, Agent, Method Who did what, when, how: interpretation acts, assertion decisions, alignment and retrieval activities, agents with role and trust, rules and policies.
    Record Metadata (encoding and retrieval of concrete records) (record-metadata) RecordMetadata Provenance of a concrete record rather than its content: where a record was read from, which release, when; how an artefact was serialised.
    Utilities (utility) Coding, Extension, Qualifier Reusable datatypes (codings, extensions, qualifiers).
