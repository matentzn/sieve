    Sieve - Evidence Packets for Assertion Curation¶

    A model for evidence-based curation of semantic assertions (ontology axioms, knowledge graph triples, etc.).

    Core concept: An EvidencePacket bundles a statement with evidence supporting or disputing it, plus curation workflow status.

    Evidence model aligned with SEPIO (Scientific Evidence and Provenance Information Ontology).

    URI: https://w3id.org/sieve

    Name: sieve
