    Class: AssertionDecision¶

    The overall decision that puts a claim into Mondo (or keeps it out). It is made over all lines and recorded with the score, rule and packet version it was given on. Every claim rests on a human decision: per claim (sign-off, consensus, batch) or per policy (delegated, provider authority). Automated routes can only accept.
